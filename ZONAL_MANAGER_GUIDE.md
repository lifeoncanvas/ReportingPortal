## 1. Portal Access
- **Portal URL:** Access the link provided by your administration team (e.g., `https://<portal-domain>/` or your local deployment URL).
- **Supported Browsers:** Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Safari (desktop and mobile browsers supported).

---

## 2. Account Creation (Sign Up)

If you do not have an account yet, navigate to the portal login screen and click **"Sign up here →"** at the bottom.

### Method 1: One-Click KingsChat Sign Up (Recommended)
> **Why KingsChat?** Fast, secure, and requires no separate passwords.

1. On the registration page, click the gold **"USE KINGSCHAT"** button.
2. A secure KingsChat authorization window will pop up.
3. If not already logged into KingsChat in your browser, enter your KingsChat mobile number / username and confirm the prompt or OTP sent to your KingsChat mobile app.
4. Click **"Authorize" / "Allow"** to link your profile.
5. Your account is automatically registered and submitted for Administrator approval.

---

### Method 2: Email Address Sign Up
1. On the registration page, click **"USE EMAIL ADDRESS"**.
2. Complete the required fields:
   - **Full Name:** Your full name (e.g., *Pastor John Doe*).
   - **Email Address:** Your active official email address.
   - **Create Password:** Enter a secure password (minimum 6 characters).
3. **Security Questions (for Account Recovery):**
   - Provide answers to 3 recovery questions:
     1. *Mother's maiden name?*
     2. *First school name?*
     3. *Favorite pet name?*
   > 💡 *Note: Keep your security answers safe. You will be able to recover your account using these answers if you ever forget your password.*
4. Click **"Register with Email"**.
5. You will see a confirmation screen: *"Registration Successful! Your account has been created and is pending admin approval."*

---

### Method 3: Phone Number Sign Up
1. On the registration page, click **"USE PHONE NUMBER"**.
2. Complete the required fields:
   - **Full Name:** Your full name.
   - **Phone Number:** Your mobile number with international code.
   - **Create Password:** Enter a secure password.
3. Answer the 3 recovery security questions.
4. Click **"Register with Phone"**.
5. You will receive confirmation that your account is registered and pending admin approval.

---

### Method 4: Administrator Email Invitation
If your administrator sent you an invitation link directly:
1. Open the invitation email received in your inbox.
2. Click the **"Accept Invitation / Complete Setup"** link.
3. Enter your **Full Name** and choose your **Password**.
4. Once submitted, your account will be active immediately.

---

## 3. Account Approval & Activation
To maintain reporting integrity and data security:
- All newly created accounts require a one-time approval by the **System Administrator**.
- Once approved, your role will be assigned as **Zonal Manager** with access to your respective zone/region.
- You will receive an email confirmation once your account has been approved and activated.
- If you attempt to log in before approval, you will see the notice:
  > *"Your account is pending admin approval. Please contact an administrator."*

---

## 4. Signing In (Login)

Once your account has been approved, you can log in using any of the following methods:

### Option A: Sign In with KingsChat (Fastest)
1. Navigate to the login page.
2. Click **"SIGN IN WITH KINGSCHAT"**.
3. Authorize with your KingsChat account.
4. You will be automatically redirected directly into the **Zonal Manager Dashboard**.

### Option B: Sign In with Email
1. Click **"SIGN IN WITH EMAIL"** to expand the email login form.
2. Enter your registered **Email Address** and **Password**.
3. Click **"Sign In"**.

### Option C: Sign In with Phone
1. Click **"SIGN IN WITH PHONE"** to expand the phone login form.
2. Enter your registered **Phone Number** and **Password**.
3. Click **"Sign In"**.

---

## 5. Password Recovery & Security

If you forget your password, click **"Forgot password?"** on the login page. You have two convenient recovery routes:

### Route 1: One-Time Password (OTP) via Email
1. Enter your registered email address.
2. Click **"Send Verification Code"**.
3. Check your email for the 6-digit OTP code (valid for 10 minutes).
4. Enter the code and set your **New Password**.

### Route 2: Security Questions Recovery
1. If you cannot access your email, select **"Verify via Security Questions"**.
2. Answer the 3 security questions you set during registration.
3. Once verified, set your new password immediately.

## 6. Navigating the Portal as a Zonal Manager

Once logged in, you will have access to the following sections from the sidebar:

### 1. Dashboard (`/zonal/dashboard`)
- **Key Metrics:** View overall summaries of submitted reports, approvals, and weekly activities.
- **Recent Activities:** Track pending submissions and feedback.

### 2. Reporting Portal (`/zonal/reporting`)
- Submit and view weekly and periodic reports including:
  - **Zonal Master Report & Information:** Weekly attendance, meetings, remitted partnership figures, etc.
  - **Healing Outreaches:** Number of outreaches, attendees, and impacts.
  - **Magazine Distribution:** Adult, Teens, and Kids Healing to the Nations magazine orders.
  - **Testimonies:** Record and upload miraculous testimonies and praise reports.
  - **Partnership Remittances:** Track church and zonal remittance contributions.
- **Media Uploads:** Attach photos, video clips, and documents (`.pdf`, `.jpg`, `.mp4`).
- **Download & Export:** Export your submitted reports to PDF or Excel for your records.

### 3. Notifications (`/zonal/notifications`)
- Real-time alerts when:
  - A report is approved.
  - The Global/Admin team requests clarification on a submitted report.
  - Important administrative announcements are posted.

### 4. Settings (`/zonal/settings`)
- Update your display name and contact details.
- Change your account password anytime.
- Update your security questions for account recovery.
- Toggle dark/light theme and preferred language.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

#### Q: The page says "Your account is pending admin approval".
**Answer:** This is normal for new registrations. Please notify your Head of Administration or Portal Administrator to approve your account. Once approved, you can log in immediately.

#### Q: KingsChat login popup didn't appear or closed automatically.
**Answer:**
1. Check that your browser is not blocking popups. Look for a popup blocker icon in your browser address bar and choose *"Always allow popups from this site"*.
2. Ensure you have an active internet connection.
3. If using a mobile browser, open KingsChat first to ensure you are signed in.

#### Q: I forgot my password and haven't received an email OTP.
**Answer:**
1. Check your **Spam / Junk** folder for the verification email.
2. Alternatively, select **"Answer Security Questions"** on the forgot password screen to reset your password without waiting for an email.
3. If you still encounter issues, contact your portal administrator to reset your credentials.

#### Q: Can I use KingsChat if I originally registered with email?
**Answer:** Yes! If your KingsChat account is registered with the same email or phone number, the portal automatically links your KingsChat identity to your existing account upon signing in with KingsChat.
